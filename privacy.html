StoryStrand Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

StoryStrand is a book library app that organizes your books by author and series. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Information we collect

StoryStrand does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal information. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no advertising or analytics.

Your library

The books you add or import (from CSV, text, Word, or PDF files) are stored on your own device. Imported files are read on your device and are not uploaded to us.

Book lookups

To find covers, page counts, and other book details, the app sends book information such as titles, authors, or ISBNs to the Google Books service. That service is run by Google and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

Children

StoryStrand is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
